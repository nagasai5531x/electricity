Project Defense & Viva Q&A Cheat Sheet

Q1: What is the main objective of this mini project?

To transform raw smart meter time-series data into actionable insights using interactive visualization, enabling consumers and facility managers to identify peak load periods, detect energy waste, and optimize tariff costs.

Q2: Why was Tableau chosen for visualization?

Tableau provides enterprise-grade data blending, interactive filter actions, high-throughput aggregation of large time-series datasets, and dynamic Story Points for stakeholder presentations.

Q3: How are energy anomalies detected?

Through statistical z-score thresholds on hourly active power (kW) normalized by sector and season. Any deviation exceeding 2.5 standard deviations above the sector mean is flagged as an anomaly.

Q4: How does Time-of-Use (TOU) tariff modeling benefit users?

It models differential pricing between peak and off-peak periods, demonstrating that shifting even 15% of flexible appliance loads (e.g. EV charging, water heaters) yields 12-18% reductions in electricity expenditure.
